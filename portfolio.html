A bit about how I work

I enjoy turning inefficient, manual processes into practical software that people find useful. That interest led me to full-stack development, where I could bring ideas to life, and eventually sparked my curiosity about what happens beneath the surface, drawing me towards explainable AI.

Beyond coding, I enjoy bringing people together and organising events. It's taught me that shipping good software and running a good event have a lot in common - both come down to planning early, communicating clearly, and staying calm when something breaks two hours before the deadline.
